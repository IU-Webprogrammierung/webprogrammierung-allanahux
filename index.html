Hi there!

I’m Allanah, 26, originally from the north of Germany near Hamburg. In 2022 I moved to Mannheim to be with my partner, and over time I discovered a new sense of home in the south. Photography has always been part of my world. My grandfather collected cameras and passed on his love for capturing moments. As a teenager I occasionally experimented with his old analog models, but it wasn’t until 2024 that I truly dared to step into photography myself. Since then, it has become the way I slow down, notice details, and capture the subtle atmosphere of a moment. Design is the other path I’ve chosen. In 2022, I began studying User Experience Design, fascinated by the balance between creativity, psychology, and human needs. Today, both photography and design shape how I see the world – and how I create within it.

UX PROJECTS

Pilates Booking App • Reformé

This project explores a concept app for the new Pilates studio “Reformé Active” in Mannheim. It promotes movement, balance, and mindful living. The app streamlines class bookings and improves the overall user journey.

Gardening Community • Bee Friends

Bee Friends is a community app that inspires mindful gardening and supports bees. Through small actions and weekly challenges, users grow together and help nature thrive.

Bike Monitoring • SpinSafe

SpinSafe offers a smart solution to improve both comfort and bike safety. Designed for road cyclists, it detects issues early and helps prevent unexpected failures.

Hotel • Villa Limoncina

Villa Limoncina is a serene retreat on a lemon farm in the hills of Tuscany. Surrounded by citrus gardens and soft breezes, guests reconnect with calm and creativity. The website reflects this refreshing atmosphere through light design and a sense of balance.

Fleet Management • avanext

avanext is a concept for modern fleet management software, designed to simplify complex data and support efficient, everyday decision-making for mobility teams.

INSPIRATIONS

I find inspiration in many small things around me. In the rhythm of everyday life — the pattern of bricks on a wall, the texture of a surface, and most of all, in colors. The soft glow of a sunset, the deep tones of the forest, the vibrant green of a golf course. I’m fascinated by the way light moves — how it slips between objects, lands on walls, and meets the shadows. These moments, often fleeting, make me pause and look closer. They remind me how much beauty lives in the ordinary, waiting to be seen.
